# Voice and clarity

Use these checks once, proportionate to the edit. A user's sample and project voice control style.

1. Preserve facts, qualifications, intent, and meaningful detail. Do not invent testimonials, personal reactions, quotes, claims, or evidence to make prose livelier.
2. Use concrete language and direct verbs. Remove empty praise, vague attribution and unsupported significance; retain deliberate phrasing that belongs to the author.
3. Match the author's vocabulary, rhythm and level of formality. Do not ban ordinary words, punctuation or sentence patterns merely because an AI might use them.
4. Give each sentence a useful job. Remove repeated claims and unnecessary setup without forcing uniform paragraphs, slogans, jokes or dramatic fragments.
5. Read the result for clarity and fidelity. Stop when the requested change is achieved; another stylistic pass needs a specific unresolved problem.

For a small correction, leave unrelated text intact. Normal assistant replies and internal analysis do not require this marketing-copy workflow unless the user specifically requests a writing pass.
