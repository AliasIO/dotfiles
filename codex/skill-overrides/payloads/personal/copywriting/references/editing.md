# Editing and content refresh

Start with the user's requested improvement and the existing draft. A spelling fix or shorter sentence should stay small. A broader review can use the following dimensions where relevant:

- Meaning and clarity: preserve the proposition, qualifications, intended reader, and sequence needed to understand it.
- Voice: match the author's sample and project conventions; do not replace distinctive writing with a generic conversion formula.
- Evidence: verify factual claims against supplied sources or the authoritative project context. Flag unsupported specifics rather than embellishing them.
- Benefit and relevance: explain a capability's actual consequence when it helps the reader. Technical detail may itself be the benefit for a technical audience.
- Action: make the requested next step and its consequences understandable without inventing urgency or removing necessary conditions.
- Consistency: align names, prices, plan limits, links, spelling and promises across the changed surface.

For a content refresh, inspect what actually changed since publication: product behavior, screenshots, dates, links, evidence or audience questions. Update those parts and any dependent claims. Do not change a publication date merely to create a freshness signal or replace unaffected sections by default.

Return the revised text or implementation appropriate to the request. Explain only material factual gaps or consequential editorial decisions. End with the brief [voice and clarity](voice-and-clarity.md) check; no fixed sequence of sweeps, simulated reviewers or numerical quality gate is required.
