# Marketing page and form review

Identify the page's purpose and traffic intent before evaluating it. Preserve the product's positioning and verified offer.

Inspect the dimensions that can affect the requested conversion:

- Message match: does the page deliver what the incoming link or ad promised?
- Value and comprehension: can the intended reader understand the product and whether it fits their need?
- Action hierarchy: is the primary action clear, usable and accurately labeled, with alternatives where useful?
- Evidence: are testimonials, logos, numbers, guarantees and comparisons supported and relevant?
- Objections and conditions: can a reader understand pricing, compatibility, commitment and what happens next?
- Friction and accessibility: inspect actual validation, navigation, performance, keyboard use and mobile layout before attributing a problem to wording.

Use a screenshot or rendered interaction to verify a visual claim. Conversion data should include the funnel definition, relevant segment and observation window. A heuristic identifies a candidate test; it does not establish a measured failure.

For lead/contact forms, consult [form details](form.md) only as needed. Recommend or implement the smallest useful correction. Do not require every page to hide navigation, add urgency, display a recommended plan, or include a sales CTA.
