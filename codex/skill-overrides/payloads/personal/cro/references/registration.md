# Registration and trial signup

Define the registration success event and the business/identity requirements before changing fields or verification. Measure completion alongside activation, qualified conversion and abuse/support effects when relevant.

## Field and authentication decisions

Keep required information that is necessary at this stage; defer optional information only when the downstream workflow permits it. Field removal, multi-step forms, passwordless access and social login are hypotheses, not universal improvements. Preserve the established identity model unless its change is requested.

Use visible labels, appropriate input/autocomplete types, accessible errors and accurate help text. Allow password-manager use and paste. Show password requirements before failure and permit visibility toggles when appropriate. Do not silently relax security rules or disclose account existence beyond the project's established policy.

Choose one or multiple steps from task complexity and observed behavior, not an arbitrary field-count threshold. In a multi-step flow, preserve safe inputs, support back navigation, show truthful progress, and handle interruption/retry without duplicate accounts or charges. Never persist passwords in unsafe browser storage to save progress.

## Recovery and validation

Check invalid input, failed requests, duplicate submissions, existing accounts, expired links, cancelled social sign-in, and the successful next step where relevant to the change. Preserve input that can safely be retained. Provide a legitimate recovery path without inventing a promise of automatic sign-in.

For a mobile change, inspect the actual keyboard/input behavior, focus, error placement, tap targets and overflow at the project's target viewport. Compare the same funnel population and observation window before claiming an improvement.

Copy such as “no credit card,” “free forever,” time estimates and privacy promises must match actual behavior and terms. Use the activation reference only if the issue continues after account creation.
