# AI-search visibility

Use only when the request concerns AI-search answers, citations or agent-readable content. Start with the ordinary SEO audit's checks for accessible, useful, accurate content; do not create a separate optimization program by default.

## Observe before changing

Select representative queries tied to the user's audience and decision. Record the engine/product, date, locale, account/personalization context, exact query, answer and cited pages that can actually be observed. Distinguish brand mentions, citations, referral visits and conversions. A single answer or missing referral is not proof of stable visibility or absence.

Compare supported pages and competitor evidence on the same basis. Investigate incorrect or stale claims, inaccessible content, weak primary evidence or unclear product information. Use readable structure, accurate entities and original sources where they improve comprehension. Do not add arbitrary passage lengths, a fixed number of questions, or duplicate AI-only prose solely to satisfy a ranking hypothesis.

## Claims and emerging formats

Verify current target-engine guidance before relying on its retrieval behavior, crawler policy or reporting capabilities. Do not claim a guaranteed citation, rank, traffic or conversion lift. [Google's AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-features) and the target provider's official documentation are starting points, not a substitute for current evidence.

The [2024 GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) describes experimental visibility results under its own benchmarks. Quote a result only after checking the exact method, metric, baseline and evaluation setting; do not transfer its percentages to present-day engines as expected outcomes.

Treat `llms.txt`, Open Knowledge Format, machine-readable page variants and commerce-agent protocols as optional integrations. Verify the primary specification and an actual consumer/use case first. Parsability does not establish a ranking benefit. Keep human-visible product facts authoritative and avoid maintaining duplicate facts without a concrete benefit.

## Delivery

Report observed evidence separately from hypotheses. Recommend the smallest correction or bounded experiment, with the outcome and observation window that would support it. Preserve ordinary crawl/indexing checks and valid structured data; use `schema` for implementation and verify search-feature eligibility separately.
